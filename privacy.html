Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Dark Horse AI is an AI strategy and automation consultancy run by Adham Hamza in London, United Kingdom. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Questions go to adham@thedarkhorseai.com.

What we collect

  • Website analytics. We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to see how people use this site (pages visited, scroll depth, clicks, device type). They set cookies and process data under their own privacy policies.
  • The intake form. If you fill it in, we keep what you send (name, email, business details, your answers) in our CRM so we can reply and prepare for a call.
  • Calls and email. If you book or join a call or email us, we keep the details needed to follow up. Calls may be recorded with your knowledge to produce notes.
  • Our social media tools. We use our own software, connected through the official APIs of Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, to publish videos to Dark Horse AI's own accounts and to read public comments left on those posts so we can reply. We see only what those platforms make available to an account owner: your public username or name, your comment, and when you posted it. We do not access your account, your messages, or anything private.

How we use it

To answer you, to run and improve our services, and to understand which content is useful. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Where it is stored

With the service providers we use to run the business (for example our CRM, email, calendar and hosting providers), each under its own security and privacy terms. Some of them are outside the UK; where that happens we rely on their standard contractual protections.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and CRM records: as long as we have a working relationship, then up to 2 years. Comment data collected by our social media tools: up to 12 months. Analytics: per the providers' default retention.

Your rights and data deletion

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. To have your data deleted, including anything our social media tools collected from your comments, email adham@thedarkhorseai.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the username or email it relates to. We confirm deletion within 30 days. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
